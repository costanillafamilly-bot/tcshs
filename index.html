WHO WE ARE

Where curiosity becomes possibility.

Toledo City Science High School is a community committed to developing students through science, research, academic excellence, leadership, and meaningful service.

Beyond lessons and laboratories, the school provides opportunities for students to discover their interests, develop their skills, collaborate with others, and contribute to the community.
